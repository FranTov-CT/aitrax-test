rusty violins echo through the cathedral of forgotten socks
a comet left its luggage on the doorstep of the moon
the baker kneaded shadows into a loaf of quiet bread
twelve turtles argued politics beneath the willow tree
her laughter cracked the ice on the sleeping reservoir
a typewriter dreamed of becoming an orchestra someday
the lighthouse blinked twice and the fog took the hint
cinnamon rivers wind through the valley of spare buttons
the owl misplaced its glasses somewhere near September
every puddle remembers the shape of a passing cloud
