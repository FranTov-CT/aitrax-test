the river kept a diary written entirely in ripples
a staircase spiraled into a rumor about the thirteenth floor
pistachio dusk settled over the carnival of tired kites
the dictionary lost the word for Tuesday and panicked quietly
seven sailors mapped a coastline made of melting candy
the violinist tuned her bow to the frequency of old letters
a scarecrow applied for the position of village philosopher
marmalade sunlight dripped slowly off the windowsill
the elevator music auditioned for a role in the thunderstorm
somewhere a doorknob dreamed of becoming a planet
