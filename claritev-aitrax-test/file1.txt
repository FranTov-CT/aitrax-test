violet harbor drifts beyond the quiet meridian line
copper lanterns hum against a restless autumn sky
the fox counted seven pebbles near the mossy well
gravity folds softly where the old bridge used to stand
whispering reeds trade secrets with the midnight tide
a crooked compass points toward forgotten marmalade
thunder rehearses its lines behind the velvet curtain
sixteen paper boats raced the gutter after the storm
she traded her umbrella for a jar of amber fireflies
the clock forgot to chime and nobody seemed to mind
